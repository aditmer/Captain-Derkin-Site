FREIGHT DECK PRIVACY

Captain Derkin: Freight Deck · Last updated July 28, 2026

Ditmer Partners ("we," "us," or "our") provides Captain Derkin: Freight Deck (the "Game"). This policy explains how the Game handles information.

Summary

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • Optional anonymous playtest analytics are off by default.
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, precise location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or free-form text through the Game.
  • We do not use analytics data for advertising.
  • Analytics can be disabled at any time in the Game's System menu.

Information we collect

If you choose to enable playtest analytics, the Game may collect:

  • a randomly generated anonymous installation identifier;
  • a randomly generated playtest-session identifier;
  • gameplay interactions, such as run starts and outcomes, purchases and sales made with in-game currency, travel, contracts, upgrades, encounters, guidance shown, and failed actions;
  • game state needed to interpret those events, such as scenario, captain, run day, star system, balance version, and anonymous run seed; and
  • app and build metadata, such as app version, build version, update identifier, and platform.

The Game does not intentionally collect your name, email address, precise location, contacts, advertising identifier, typed free-form content, or session replays.

How we use information

We use optional analytics only to:

  • understand how closed-test participants use the Game;
  • balance the in-game economy, difficulty, encounters, and progression;
  • identify confusing or frustrating parts of the experience; and
  • improve reliability and future versions of the Game.

Legal basis and your choice

Playtest analytics are collected only after you opt in. They are disabled by default. You can withdraw your consent at any time from System → Playtest analytics. Disabling analytics stops future analytics collection and resets the Game's local anonymous analytics identity.

Service providers

We use PostHog as a service provider to process optional analytics on our behalf. PostHog processes this information under our instructions so that we can analyze and improve the Game. We do not sell analytics data or use it for third-party advertising.

Data security and retention

Analytics are transmitted using encrypted connections. We retain analytics only for as long as reasonably necessary for closed testing, balancing, product improvement, security, and legal obligations, after which they are deleted or de-identified.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the limited, anonymous data involved.

Data deletion requests

Because the Game does not use accounts and analytics are associated with random identifiers, we may not be able to connect stored analytics to a particular person without the relevant anonymous identifier. You may contact us to request deletion of analytics associated with an identifier you provide at apps@ditmer.us.

Children

The Game is intended for people age 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

International processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international processing.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Game or our practices change. We will update the "Last updated" date when we make changes.

Contact us

Questions or requests about this policy can be sent to Ditmer Partners at apps@ditmer.us.
