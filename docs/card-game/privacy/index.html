CARD GAME PRIVACY

Captain Derkin · Last updated October 7, 2025

Ditmer Partners ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Captain Derkin mobile application (the "Service"). This policy explains the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use the Service.

Information we collect

Information you provide

Captain Derkin does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to play.

Automatically collected information

When you use Captain Derkin, we may automatically collect:

  • device type and operating-system version;
  • game progress and statistics stored locally on your device;
  • crash reports and diagnostic data; and
  • anonymous usage analytics used to understand feature usage.

How we use information

  • to provide and maintain the Service;
  • to improve performance and fix bugs;
  • to understand how players use the game; and
  • to respond to support requests.

Data storage and security

Your game progress is stored locally on your device. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the limited data involved, although no electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Third-party services

Captain Derkin may use third-party services for analytics, advertising, distribution, and app functionality, including Apple App Store services, Google Play services, and advertising providers. Those services may process device or usage information under their own privacy policies.

Advertising and rewards

Captain Derkin may offer optional ad-supported rewards. Watching reward ads is voluntary. Advertising providers may process device information to deliver and measure advertising. Personalized advertising choices can be managed through your device settings where available.

Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information may contact us.

Data retention and your choices

We retain automatically collected information only as long as needed to provide and improve the Service or meet legal obligations. You can remove locally stored game progress by uninstalling the app. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to access, delete, or restrict certain processing.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game or our practices change. The "Last updated" date will be revised when we do.

Contact us

Privacy questions or requests can be sent to apps@ditmer.us.
